观测控制与电控系统

天语望远镜电控系统采用分布式控制架构，负责望远镜方位轴、高度轴以及调焦机构的高精度运动控制。 系统由直驱力矩电机、伺服驱动器、绝对式光栅码盘和高性能运动控制器等组成。

分布式控制架构

方位轴与高度轴均采用力矩电机直接驱动，减少机械传动环节，提高系统响应速度和跟踪稳定性。 各轴控制单元通过分布式架构协同工作，为望远镜指向、跟踪和调焦提供底层控制基础。

位置反馈与闭环控制

望远镜采用雷尼绍绝对式光栅码盘提供高精度位置反馈。高性能运动控制器根据编码器数据执行 PID 闭环控制， 实时修正方位轴和高度轴的位置及速度误差。

主要控制性能

  • 机架形式：高精度地平式机架
  • 驱动方式：力矩电机直接驱动
  • 方位转动范围：±360°
  • 高度转动范围：0–90°
  • 电动调焦精度：≤ 0.01 mm
  • 指向精度：RMS ≤ 5″
  • 跟踪精度：5 分钟内 ≤ 0.3″ RMS

上层观测调度、环境联锁和自动化观测功能将根据设备接入与系统联调进展持续完善。

Observation and Electrical Control System

The Tianyu Telescope uses a distributed electrical control architecture for high-precision control of the azimuth axis, elevation axis, and motorized focusing mechanism. The system consists of direct-drive torque motors, servo drives, absolute optical encoders, and a high-performance motion controller.

Distributed Control Architecture

Both the azimuth and elevation axes are directly driven by torque motors, reducing mechanical transmission components and improving system response and tracking stability. Distributed axis-control units work together to provide the low-level foundation for telescope pointing, tracking, and focusing.

Position Feedback and Closed-Loop Control

Renishaw absolute optical encoders provide high-precision position feedback. The motion controller uses the encoder measurements in a PID closed-loop control system to correct position and velocity errors on the azimuth and elevation axes in real time.

Main Control Performance

  • Mount type: High-precision altitude-azimuth mount
  • Drive system: Direct-drive torque motors
  • Azimuth range: ±360°
  • Elevation range: 0–90°
  • Motorized focusing accuracy: ≤ 0.01 mm
  • Pointing accuracy: RMS ≤ 5 arcsec
  • Tracking accuracy: ≤ 0.3 arcsec RMS over five minutes

Higher-level scheduling, environmental interlocks, and automated observing functions will continue to be developed and validated as device integration and system commissioning progress.
