光机系统与科学相机

天语望远镜采用快速、大视场主焦点光学系统，配备高精度地平式机架和 COSMOS-66 背照式科学 CMOS 相机， 面向大视场、高时间分辨率的时间域巡天观测。

光学系统

望远镜采用主焦点结构，由直径 1000 mm 的双曲面主镜和改正透镜组组成，系统焦比为 f/1.57。 光学系统设计视场对角线为 4.5°，匹配 COSMOS-66 相机后，单次曝光可覆盖约 8.5 平方度观测天区。

主镜采用低膨胀系数微晶玻璃材料，并镀有多层金属介质膜。在 400–1000 nm 波段内， 主镜反射率高于 95%，主镜面型精度 RMS 优于 λ/50。

机械系统

望远镜采用高精度地平式机架，方位轴和高度轴均由力矩电机直接驱动。镜筒采用高刚度桁架式结构， 具有较小的重力变形，结构固有频率高于 10 Hz，有利于维持大视场成像和跟踪稳定性。

  • 方位转动范围：±360°
  • 高度转动范围：0–90°
  • 镜筒结构：高刚度桁架式结构
  • 结构固有频率：> 10 Hz
  • 电动调焦精度：≤ 0.01 mm
  • 指向精度：RMS ≤ 5″
  • 跟踪精度：5 分钟内 ≤ 0.3″ RMS

COSMOS-66 科学相机

望远镜终端配备 COSMOS-66 背照式科学 CMOS 相机。该相机与 f/1.57 主焦点光学系统匹配， 用于大视场、高时间分辨率的时间域巡天观测。

  • 探测器规模：8K × 8K
  • 像元尺寸：10 μm/pixel
  • 最小读出噪声：0.7 e⁻
  • 暗电流：0.05 e⁻/pixel/s
  • 单次观测天区：约 8.5 平方度

Optomechanical System and Scientific Camera

The Tianyu Telescope combines a fast, wide-field prime-focus optical system with a high-precision altitude-azimuth mount and a COSMOS-66 backside-illuminated scientific CMOS camera for wide-field, high-time-resolution surveys.

Optical System

The telescope adopts a prime-focus optical configuration consisting of a 1,000 mm hyperbolic primary mirror and a corrector lens assembly. The system has a focal ratio of f/1.57 and a designed diagonal field of view of 4.5°. When paired with the COSMOS-66 camera, it provides approximately 8.5 deg² of sky coverage per exposure.

The primary mirror is made of low-expansion glass-ceramic material and uses a multilayer metal-dielectric coating. Its reflectivity is above 95% over the 400–1000 nm wavelength range, and its surface figure accuracy is better than λ/50 RMS.

Mechanical System

The telescope uses a high-precision altitude-azimuth mount. Both the azimuth and elevation axes are directly driven by torque motors. The high-rigidity truss structure minimizes gravitational deformation and has a structural natural frequency above 10 Hz, helping maintain stable wide-field image quality and tracking performance.

  • Azimuth range: ±360°
  • Elevation range: 0–90°
  • Telescope tube: High-rigidity truss structure
  • Structural natural frequency: > 10 Hz
  • Motorized focusing accuracy: ≤ 0.01 mm
  • Pointing accuracy: RMS ≤ 5 arcsec
  • Tracking accuracy: ≤ 0.3 arcsec RMS over five minutes

COSMOS-66 Scientific Camera

The focal-plane instrument is a COSMOS-66 backside-illuminated scientific CMOS camera. It is matched to the f/1.57 prime-focus optical system for wide-field, high-time-resolution surveys.

  • Detector format: 8K × 8K
  • Pixel size: 10 μm/pixel
  • Minimum read noise: 0.7 e⁻
  • Dark current: 0.05 e⁻/pixel/s
  • Sky coverage per exposure: Approximately 8.5 deg²
