{% if is_zh %}项目概述{% else %}Project Overview{% endif %}

{% if is_zh %} 天语望远镜是上海交通大学李政道研究所与中国科学院上海天文台联合研制的时域巡天专用 1 米光学望远镜，部署于青海冷湖赛什腾山观测站。项目重点搜寻类木冷巨行星，并捕捉超新星、近地天体等瞬变天象，依托大视场、高光学效率和高精度跟踪能力开展时间域光学观测。项目名称源于李政道先生提出的“以天之语，解物之道”。 {% else %} The Tianyu Telescope is a 1-m optical telescope dedicated to time-domain surveys, jointly developed by the Tsung-Dao Lee Institute at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and the Shanghai Astronomical Observatory, Chinese Academy of Sciences. Deployed at the Saishiteng Mountain Observatory in Lenghu, Qinghai, its science program focuses on searches for cold Jupiters and on detecting supernovae, near-Earth objects, and other transient phenomena. The telescope combines a wide field of view, high optical throughput, and high-precision tracking for time-domain optical observations. Its name is inspired by Tsung-Dao Lee's idea, “From the Language of Heaven to the Rationale of Matter.” {% endif %}

{% if is_zh %}主要特色{% else %}Key Features{% endif %}

    {% if is_zh %}
  • 快速大视场光学系统：采用主焦点结构，系统焦比 f/1.57，匹配 COSMOS-66 相机后单次覆盖约 8.5 平方度天区
  • 高性能主镜：1 米口径微晶玻璃主镜在 400–1000 nm 波段的反射率高于 95%
  • 高精度机械系统：采用地平式机架和力矩电机直接驱动，指向精度 RMS ≤ 5″，5 分钟跟踪精度 ≤ 0.3″ RMS
  • 科学级 CMOS 相机：8K × 8K 背照式探测器，像元尺寸 10 μm，最小读出噪声 0.7 e⁻
    • {% else %}
  • Fast wide-field optics: a prime-focus system with an f/1.57 focal ratio and approximately 8.5 deg² sky coverage with the COSMOS-66 camera
  • High-performance primary mirror: a 1-m glass-ceramic mirror with reflectivity above 95% from 400 to 1000 nm
  • High-precision mechanics: an altitude-azimuth mount with direct-drive torque motors, RMS pointing accuracy of 5 arcsec or better, and tracking accuracy of 0.3 arcsec RMS or better over five minutes
  • Scientific CMOS detector: an 8K × 8K backside-illuminated sensor with 10 μm pixels and a minimum read noise of 0.7 e⁻
    • {% endif %}

{% if is_zh %}科学目标{% else %}Science Objectives{% endif %}

    {% if is_zh %}
  • 搜寻类木冷巨行星及其凌星信号
  • 捕捉超新星及其他快速光学瞬变事件
  • 监测近地天体及其他太阳系小天体
  • 开展大视场、高时间分辨率的长期光变监测
    • {% else %}
  • Search for cold Jupiters and their transit signals
  • Detect supernovae and other rapidly evolving optical transients
  • Observe near-Earth objects and other small Solar System bodies
  • Conduct long-term, wide-field, high-cadence photometric monitoring
    • {% endif %}
